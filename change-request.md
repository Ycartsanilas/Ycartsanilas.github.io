# Change Request

## Proposed Change
Embed a Google Map of my hometown, La Porte, Texas, on my personal project website.

## Reasons for the Change
My stakeholders, consisting of my instructor and fellow students, may be interested in learning more about my hometown and the region I call home.  Incorporating a Google Map would provide geographical context and allow them to gain a better understanding of my background and where my roots are.

## How This Change Will Add Value
Incorporating a Google Map would add value to my website by making it more interactive.  Rather than just reading about my hometown, visitors would have the opportunity to explore the area without having to leave my website.

## How This Change Will Affect the Constraints
- **Time:** This change would require approximately 35 additonal minutes to research, implement, and test the embedded Google Map, allowing extra time for troubleshooting due to my limited experience with HTML.
- **Quality:** The embedded Google Map would enhance the visual appeal of my website while making it more interactive.  Although some visitors may have difficulty navigating an interactive map, I believe it would be user-friendly for my intended audience, which consists of my instructor and fellow students.
- **Budget:** I do not forsee any additional expenses associated with this change.  The option to embed a Google Map is available at no cost, and the only resources I would need are my laptop and an internet connection, both of which I already have.
